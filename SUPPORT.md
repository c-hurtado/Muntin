# Support

Muntin is a personal project with no formal support.

- **Bugs and ideas:** open an [issue](https://github.com/c-hurtado/Muntin/issues). Include your Windows version, your monitor setup, and the log from `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Muntin\Logs` if Muntin misbehaved.
- **How do I…:** see the [README](README.md) and the [full guide](Muntin.md).

Please don't take Muntin issues to the PowerToys project: Microsoft doesn't make or support Muntin.
